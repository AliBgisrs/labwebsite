Research Areas

Our lab integrates multi-scale sensing and advanced modeling to address critical challenges in precision agriculture and natural resource management.

1. Crop Sensing & Enviromics

This research focuses on predicting crop yield and quality by leveraging multi-year trials and high-resolution UAS (Unmanned Aerial Systems) data. By integrating environmental variables with phenotypic data, we aim to build robust predictive models for modern agronomy.

2. Disease & Stress Management

We utilize pest scouting combined with specialized UAS imagery to model and manage weed populations and disease outbreaks. This proactive approach enables targeted interventions, reducing chemical use and protecting yield potential.

3. Water-Limited Production

In semi-arid regions like Montana, water efficiency is paramount. Our work employs thermal and multispectral imagery to accurately quantify crop water status, facilitating precision irrigation and drought-stress characterization.

4. Livestock & Rangeland Systems

Extending beyond crops, we combine remote sensing technologies with animal-specific data to support adaptive grazing strategies. This holistic view improves the sustainability of rangeland systems and livestock health.
