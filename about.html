Our Mission

The Ali Bazrafkan Lab at Montana State University is dedicated to bridging the gap between advanced computational modeling and real-world agricultural challenges. We leverage tools like GIS, and Python to create a digital twin of the agricultural landscape.

The Research Cycle

1. Sensing

Capturing high-resolution spatial data using drones, LiDAR, and multi-spectral sensors to understand the current state of the field.

2. Modeling

Translating raw data into physics-based simulations and 3D environments to predict how agricultural systems will react to different variables.

3. Deciding

Utilizing AI and mathematical optimization to provide actionable insights that increase efficiency, sustainability, and yield.

The Montana Context

Located in the heart of the Big Sky Country, our lab is uniquely positioned to address the needs of large-scale wheat and pulse production. We work closely with local producers and stakeholders to ensure that our "Sensing to Deciding" framework is robust, scalable, and practical for the future of farming.
